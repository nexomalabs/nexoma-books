About this volume

Volume IV is the operations volume: deployment, inference optimization, security, observability, governance, cost, and the full lifecycle of a model in production.

Prompt injection, red-teaming, human-in-the-loop approval gates, model migration, and regulatory compliance are treated as core engineering concerns rather than appendices.

Mission

Run an AI system in production without it hurting anyone.

Prerequisites. Volumes I to III, or equivalent production engineering experience.
