How errata are handled

  • Every reported error is verified against the manuscript source before it is listed here.
  • Corrections are folded into the next printing and into the current EPUB and PDF as soon as they are confirmed.
  • Errors in the companion lab code are tracked as issues in the companion repository, not on this page.

Report an error

Email errata@nexomalabs.com with the volume, printing, page number, and the text as printed. A photograph or a copied line is ideal.
