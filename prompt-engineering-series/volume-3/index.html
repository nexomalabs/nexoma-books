About this volume

Volume III covers the layer where most real systems are actually built: context pipelines, retrieval, tool use, and the agent control surface.

Agents are treated as an engineering problem of control and containment rather than one of capability. Hooks, skills, subagents, tool permissions, and sandboxed execution get first-class treatment.

Mission

Give a model context, tools, memory, and autonomy — safely.

Prerequisites. Volumes I and II, or equivalent. Comfortable building and testing software.
