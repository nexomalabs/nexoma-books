About this volume

Volume II is about control. It treats a prompt as an engineered artifact with a specification, a test suite, and a regression history — not as a phrase you tune until the output looks right.

It introduces the series' signature methodology: the AI Review Board and the Critique Board, two distinct evaluation structures that answer different questions about a system you are about to ship.

Mission

Make a model do what you specify, provably — through systematic prompt design, structured outputs, evaluation, and the Review Board methodology.

Prerequisites. Volume I, or equivalent working knowledge of LLMs. Programming experience in at least one language.
